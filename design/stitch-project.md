- 61 of 61 downloads succeeded; `design/previews/` is 13 MB.
- HTML/code for every screen is available as `htmlCode.name` on the same resources. It was **not** copied into the app and is not stored locally; treat it as a visual reference only.
- `list_screens` and `get_project().screenInstances` both cap at 25 entries while the project holds 61 screens, so the index above was assembled from the `generate_screen_from_text` responses and verified one by one with `get_screen`.
